Our Story

Built By Travellers, For Travellers

Siona Tours started with a simple frustration — planning a group trip shouldn't mean fifty browser tabs, confusing quotes and last-minute surprises. We set out to make it as easy as sending one WhatsApp message.

Today, we design curated group tours and holiday packages across India's most loved destinations — from the snowy heights of Kashmir and Ladakh to the sun-soaked beaches of Goa and Andaman — plus a growing list of international escapes. Every itinerary is handpicked, every stay is vetted, and every question gets a real, quick answer.

How We Got Here

2013

The First Trip

Siona Tours began with a single Himachal group tour for 14 travellers — and a lot of word-of-mouth referrals after.

2017

Expanding Across India

Added Kashmir, Rajasthan, Kerala and Goa to our portfolio, along with a dedicated in-house planning team.

2021

Going International

Launched our first international packages — Dubai, Bali and Maldives — for travellers ready to go further.

Today

15,000+ Happy Travellers

28+ curated packages across India and abroad, with new destinations added every season.
